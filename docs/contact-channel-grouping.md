# Contact grouping across Sales Chatter channels

Saved contacts are selected by staff. Once linked, active authorized conversations share one Inbox entry per company, branch and saved contact. Navigation includes every linked channel with a messaging account, including providers added later. Each adapter controls sending, and every reply retains its original conversation destination.

Website phone grouping applies only to unlinked orders. A submitted phone is not a saved-contact association. Linking uses the contact's canonical display name; each invoice keeps its submitted customer name, immutable snapshot and original references. The Website timeline includes Website orders only; navigation can switch to Telegram, WhatsApp and other linked channels.

The link dialog shows the complete affected conversation list, count and current conversation/identity contacts. Website supplies every order in the displayed group, independently of message pagination. Confirmation checks a fingerprint covering membership, target contact and identity states, locks the branch, accounts, target, conversations and identities, and validates every member before mutation. Website intake also locks/touches the branch, so a concurrent order arrival invalidates a stale transaction. A failed operation rolls back all links and audits. Conflicts require manager/admin review and explicit resolution. Already-correct associations do not produce duplicate audits.

Existing verified-phone matching stays limited to Telegram and WhatsApp. Website and future channels do not inherit automatic phone matching. Unlinking retains inactive identities, preventing refresh from restoring a removed link. New Website orders with the same submitted phone remain unlinked until reviewed.
